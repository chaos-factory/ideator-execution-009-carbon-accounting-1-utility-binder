Feature deep dives

Column Mapper and Unit Converter

Map your CSV columns to standard fields: Date, Site Name, ZIP Code, Usage, Units, Fuel Type. The built-in unit converter handles:

  • Electricity: kWh, MWh, GWh
  • Gas: therms, MCF, CCF, cubic meters, MMBtu

All conversions are deterministic and documented in the calculation log.

ZIP → eGRID subregion lookup

Utility Binder automatically maps ZIP codes to EPA eGRID2023 subregions and displays the applicable emission factor:

eGRID2023 RMPA • 0.498 kg CO₂e/kWh • Revision: Jun 12, 2025

If a ZIP maps to multiple subregions, the tool prompts you to select the correct one.

Scope 2 basis selector and T&D losses toggle

Choose your reporting basis and optionally include transmission and distribution losses:

  • Location-based: Uses grid average emission factors from eGRID
  • Market-based: Uses supplier-specific or contractual factors (if available)
  • Both: Exports separate columns for each basis

Location-based formula:

Emissions = Usage (kWh) × eGRID_EF × (1 + T&D_loss_factor)

Market-based formula:

Emissions = Usage (kWh) × Market_EF × (1 + T&D_loss_factor)

T&D losses default to EPA's 5% factor but can be customized per the GHG Protocol.

Gas normalization and factors

Natural gas bills come in various units. Utility Binder normalizes to standard units and applies EPA Emission Factors Hub 2025 values:

  • Natural gas EF: 53.06 kg CO₂e/MMBtu (AR5-100 GWP basis)
  • Supported units: therms, MCF, CCF, cubic meters, MMBtu

All conversions and factors are cited in the calculation log and export footers.

Validations and warnings panel

Utility Binder validates data quality and flags issues before calculation:

  • ❌ Row 14: ZIP is not 5 digits.
  • ⚠️ Row 22: Usage value is negative or zero.
  • ℹ️ Market‑based EF missing; showing location‑based only.
  • ⚠️ Row 8: Date format not recognized; using fallback.

Each message includes the row number and actionable guidance.

Factors and versioning

Utility Binder locks in specific factor versions to ensure reproducible results:

Factor Registries

  • EPA eGRID2023: Released April 2025, data year 2023, revision Jun 12, 2025
  • EPA Emission Factors Hub 2025: Natural gas and other stationary combustion
  • GWP values: IPCC AR5-100 year basis (default); AR6 available as option

All factor versions are embedded in exports and calculation logs. No manual citation needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Utility Binder runs entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no login, no tracking.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens client-side. Your bills never leave your device.

What file formats are supported?

CSV and Excel (.xlsx, .xls). Most utility bill exports work out of the box.

Can I save my work and come back later?

Yes. Use "Save Project" to download a .json file. Later, use "Load Project" to restore your session.

Which emission factor versions does it use?

EPA eGRID2023 (revision Jun 12, 2025) for electricity, EPA Emission Factors Hub 2025 for gas, and IPCC AR5-100 GWPs. All versions are locked and cited in exports.

What if my ZIP code maps to multiple eGRID subregions?

The tool will prompt you to select the correct subregion based on your utility provider.

Does it handle market-based Scope 2?

Yes. You can provide supplier-specific emission factors or RECs. If not available, the tool falls back to location-based and flags the row.

Is this compliant with California SB 253 or EU CSRD?

Utility Binder provides the calculation foundation and labels. For legal compliance, consult qualified advisors. The tool's exports are designed to support disclosure requirements.

Can I use this for Scope 1 emissions?

Natural gas combustion (included) is Scope 1. For other Scope 1 sources (fleet, refrigerants), consider pairing with a broader tool or consultant.

What browsers are supported?

Modern versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Is there a limit on file size or row count?

The tool handles 10,000+ rows efficiently. Very large files may take a few seconds to process depending on your device.

Can I customize emission factors?

Beta version uses EPA defaults. A future "Consultant Pack" will allow custom factor overrides with documentation.
